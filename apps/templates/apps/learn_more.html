About Our Project

House Price Prediction is a machine learning based web application designed to estimate house prices using important property details. It helps users get an estimated price quickly and easily.

Our Features

🏠 Price Prediction

Get an estimated house price using area, bedrooms and bathrooms.

👤 User Account

Create an account and securely log in to use the application.

📷 House Plan Upload

Upload a house image or plan along with your property details.

📊 Prediction History

View your previous predictions and manage your history.

How It Works

1. Register or log in to your account.
2. Enter the house area, bedrooms and bathrooms.
3. Add other property details and upload a house plan if needed.
4. Click the Predict button to get an estimated price.
5. View your previous predictions in the History page.

Note: The uploaded image is stored with your prediction. The current prediction model uses area, bedrooms and bathrooms; it does not analyse the image.

Technology Used

Frontend: HTML, CSS
Backend: Python, Django
Machine Learning: Linear Regression
Database: SQLite
